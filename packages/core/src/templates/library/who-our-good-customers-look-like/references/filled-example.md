# One filled in

An invented example. Copy the shape, not the content.

```
Best fit          50–500 staff · ops-heavy · UK or NL
Signs off         Head of Ops, COO, Finance Director
Worth a call when hiring ops roles · new location · switching systems
Not for us        under 50 staff — nobody owns the process yet
                  agencies — they resell, they don't operate
                  anyone mid-implementation of a competitor — wrong 18 months
```

## Why each line is written that way

**Best fit** has three clauses and no adjectives. "Ops-heavy" is checkable from a careers page.
"Ambitious" is not.

**Signs off** lists titles, not seniority. The COO signs; the CEO forwards it to the COO.

**Worth a call when** lists events, not attributes. "Hiring ops roles" happened on a date. "Growing
fast" is a vibe, and a vibe cannot be dated.

**Not for us** carries the reason inline. Six months from now nobody remembers why agencies were
cut, and someone puts them back.
