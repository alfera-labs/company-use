# What counts as a reason to call

A reason to call is something that **happened**, on a **date**, that changes what the company
needs. Everything else is a fact about the company.

## Counts

- Hired into the function you sell to, in the last six months.
- Opened, moved or closed a location.
- Announced a funding round, an acquisition, or being acquired.
- Publicly switched a system you plug into or replace.
- Posted a job that names the problem you solve.
- Their published prices, hours, capacity or menu changed.

## Does not count

- Company size, sector, or how long they have existed.
- "Growing fast", "innovative", "market leader" — an adjective off their own homepage.
- A LinkedIn post with no event behind it.
- Anything you cannot put a month against.

## Writing the line

One sentence, past tense, with the month and year, and where you saw it. If you cannot write the
month, you do not have a reason to call — leave it blank and say so.
