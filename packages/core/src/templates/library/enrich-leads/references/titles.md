# Which job title to pick

Take the first match going down this list. Stop at the first one you find — do not collect three
and pick the most senior, because the most senior person is usually the wrong one to email first.

1. **Head of Operations, Operations Director, COO** — owns the process, feels the pain daily.
2. **Finance Director, Head of Finance** — when the job is about money moving.
3. **Office Manager, Practice Manager, Studio Manager** — in companies under 50 people this is
   often the person who actually runs everything.
4. **Founder, Managing Director, CEO** — only in companies under 20 people. In a larger company
   this is a bounce.

## Never pick

- Anyone in a title containing "Intern", "Assistant to", "Trainee".
- Anyone whose profile says they left. Check the end date before you take the name.
- A generic mailbox (info@, hello@, contact@) unless there is no named person at all — and then
  say so in the notes.

## Two people look equally right

Pick the one whose tenure is longer. A person two years into the role knows where the problem is;
someone six weeks in is still finding the kitchen.
