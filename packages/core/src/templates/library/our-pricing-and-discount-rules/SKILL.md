# Our pricing and discount rules

Fill this in once. Anything that puts a number in front of a customer reads it first.

```
List price        <the number, and what it includes>
Unit              <per what: seat, month, event, hour, head>
Minimum           <the smallest job worth doing, and why>
Discount you may  <the discount that needs nobody's permission>
Discount you may not <where you stop and ask>
Never included    <the things people assume are included>
```

## Rules that do not bend

- **Never quote a number that is not in this file.** If the situation is not covered, say the
  price needs a person and stop. A wrong number said confidently is a refund.
- **A discount always has a reason and an expiry.** "10% because you're booking three" is a
  price. "10% off" is a leak.
- **Never discount to close.** If the only reason for the discount is that they hesitated, the
  answer is no.
- **Say what is not included, before they ask.** The gap between what they assumed and what they
  bought is where every bad review comes from.

## When someone asks for "your best price"

Give the list price and the one discount they qualify for. Do not open a negotiation, do not
invent a tier, and do not say "let me see what I can do".
