# What a good support reply looks like

## The shape

1. **The answer.** First line. Not "thanks for reaching out".
2. **What to do next,** if anything.
3. **Why it happened,** only if they asked or it was our fault.
4. **The apology,** only when it was ours. An apology for their own mistake is condescending.

## Never say

- "As per our policy." Say the thing the policy says.
- "The team is looking into it." Name what happens and roughly when.
- "Unfortunately." It signals a no before the sentence starts and they stop reading.

## When you do not know

Say you do not know, say who does, say when they will reply. That closes more tickets than a guess.
